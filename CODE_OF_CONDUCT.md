# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We build software that clinicians rely on to treat patients. That work needs
people who disagree well, admit mistakes quickly, and are not afraid to say "I
do not understand this" about a piece of clinical logic. We commit to making
participation in Taifa Health a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Review the change, not the author.
- Say when you are out of your depth. In clinical software, a confident guess is
  more dangerous than an admitted gap.
- Raise safety concerns early and without fear. Nobody is ever penalised for
  flagging a potential patient safety issue, including one they caused.
- Respect the confidentiality of patient data absolutely, in the codebase and
  in conversation.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it specifically.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sharing patient data, including with colleagues who have no clinical or
  operational reason to see it.
- Dismissing or discouraging a safety concern, or retaliating against someone
  who raises one.

## Scope

This applies in all org repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat,
and at any event where someone represents Taifa Health.

## Reporting

Report to `conduct@taifahealth.co.ke`. Reports are handled confidentially by
the maintainers, who will respond within 5 working days. Maintainers who cannot
act impartially on a report will recuse themselves from it.

Consequences escalate from a private warning, to a temporary ban from
participation, to permanent removal from the organisation, depending on
severity and pattern.
